How a reverse auction works

A normal auction goes up. This one goes down.

  1. You set a ceiling. The starting price is the most you are willing to pay per unit.
  2. Your suppliers compete. Each new bid has to be lower than the current best, by an amount you choose.
  3. The clock protects everyone. A bid in the last moments pushes the finish line back, so nobody wins by sniping.
  4. You award. Each item goes to one supplier, for the whole quantity — different items can go to different suppliers. Everyone gets an email either way.
