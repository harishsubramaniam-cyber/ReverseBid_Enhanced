How prices are compared

Each bidder is asked for their own freight, packaging and other costs — one figure each for the whole auction, which is how a supplier quotes them — and for the taxes on each item, as percentages. The board then ranks everyone on what you would actually pay: the bid, plus that item's share of their delivery costs, plus tax. The starting price means the most you will pay all-in, per unit.
