Accounts here are by invitation

This platform belongs to one buying organisation, so accounts are not open to sign up for.

If you are a supplier

You do not need to create anything. When a buyer invites you to an auction, the email includes a link that sets your password — that link is your way in. If you have lost it, ask your buyer contact to publish the auction again, or to send you a new invitation.

If you work for the buyer

Ask a colleague who already signs in to invite you: their name in the top bar → Your team → invite a colleague. The invitation comes by email.
